The Stay

Home base · Four nights

The Carolina Hotel

They call it the “Queen of the South.” The Carolina has 230 Four-Diamond rooms and suites, and the rooms and grand lobby were recently renovated. Breakfast is at the Carolina Dining Room, which Pinehurst bills as the best breakfast in golf, and the Coffee House off the lobby covers the quick stuff.
